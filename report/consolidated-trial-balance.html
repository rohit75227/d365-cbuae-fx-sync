Huda Beauty One row per (Company, Offset Company) intercompany relationship: Net Balance is the Company's own receivable-from-Offset-Company account netted against its own payable-to-Offset-Company account (if it holds one) — both from the Company's own books; Offset Net Balance is the same netting done from the Offset Company's own books against the Company Only covers relationships between the 16 tracked operating entities — balances against Kayali (a related but separate brand/legal group) or any other related party outside those 16 are not shown here Difference = Net Balance + Offset Net Balance — a nonzero difference flags an intercompany imbalance to investigate (including one side booked without the other), not a data error The Company filter always shows the selected company in the Company column (regardless of which side booked which account) and lists every counterparty it has a relationship with Click Details to break each Net Balance / Offset Net Balance down by the transaction currency it was actually posted in (native, unconverted amounts) — independent of the Reporting/Accounting toggle above
