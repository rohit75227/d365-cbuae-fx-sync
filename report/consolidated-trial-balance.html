Huda Beauty Aging buckets are computed from each open invoice's due date against the selected As on Date — 0-30/31-60/61-90/91-180/180+ are days PAST due; "Not Due" holds invoices whose due date hasn't arrived yet as of that date Amounts are each invoice's currently-open remaining balance (original amount less everything settled against it to date) — picking a past date changes which bucket an invoice falls into, but does not reconstruct what was actually open as of that past date (a payment made since then would still reduce the amount shown) Shown in Reporting currency (USD) only — a region or the Grand Total spans customers in companies with different local accounting currencies, which isn't meaningful to sum together Region comes from each customer's master record; customers with none set are grouped under "(No Region)" Click a region to drill into its customers; use the back link to return to All Regions
